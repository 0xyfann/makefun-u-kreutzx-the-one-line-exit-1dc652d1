Write a Python program (stdlib only) that writes exactly one funny
one-line resignation letter to /output/entry.txt (respecting the
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR env var). The line must be 1-140 characters, a single
line, not whitespace only, and original.

The joke: a specific voice — the office's only IT guy, five years on the
job — with a surprise punchline that turns his own work back on the job:
he has finally fixed his biggest bug, and the bug is the job itself.
It must read like a letter someone could actually send, with no padding
and no list of grievances, and be quotable enough for a colleague to
forward.
